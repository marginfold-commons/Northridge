# Northridge artwork

Two illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each used one request, with no variants or retries. The selected images were inspected, then converted to WebP at their original 1536 × 1024 dimensions for this portable website. These images establish atmosphere and are not maps or canonical architectural plans.

## Town entrance

File: `assets/northridge-arrival.webp`

Original prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: final raster illustration for a framed image beside homepage text on a plain static website, not a full-screen hero.
> Primary request: Northridge town entrance. Landscape 1536x1024, 3:2 aspect ratio. A quiet eye-level view of the entrance to a quietly inhabited old northeastern harbor town.
> Scene and subject: In the foreground, a weathered municipal roadside sign and one glowing old-fashioned streetlamp beside it. Soft coastal fog and a muted blue-gray evening sky. Down the road, hints of old brick buildings with warm windows. Faint harbor water and a distant lighthouse silhouette.
> Text on the roadside sign, verbatim and clearly legible, with these three lines and no other lettering:
> "WELCOME TO"
> "NORTHRIDGE"
> "Somewhere near the Marginfold."
> The third line is smaller. Spell NORTHRIDGE exactly N-O-R-T-H-R-I-D-G-E and Marginfold exactly M-a-r-g-i-n-f-o-l-d.
> Style and medium: refined painterly realism; subtle soft brush texture; ordinary civic infrastructure; cozy, elegant, slightly mysterious, modest and contemplative.
> Color and light: dusky coastal blue-grays, muted oxidized green, old brick, brass and amber lamplight.
> Composition: The entire sign and the entire lamp are wholly within the image, with generous clear margins around them so the image can be displayed uncropped. Keep the scene quiet and the town at modest human scale. The town feels maintained and inhabited although no people are visible.
> Constraints: Preserve place, not plot. No people, characters, emblems, extra invented labels, new lore or story devices. No horror, kawaii, magical effects, ornate fantasy, game screenshot, website mockup, or UI. Generate one final illustration only.

> Scene and subjects: Warm old brick; tall wooden shelves of plain books and folders; brass lamps. An old wooden desk under a green glass shaded lamp, with a dark coat draped over the chair back. Arrange these as things left during ordinary use, not a display, object collection, or product photograph.

> Color and light: dusky coastal blue-grays and muted oxidized green balanced with warm old brick, brass and amber lamplight.
> Composition: An integrated interior scene viewed from the doorway, with the desk and requested objects visible. The place is maintained and used, ordinary with slight quiet strangeness.
> Constraints: Preserve place, not plot. No lettering or readable words anywhere, including book spines, paper, notebook, walls, or signs. No people, characters, emblems, invented labels, new lore or additional story devices. No ornate fantasy clutter or excessive floating particles. No horror, kawaii, magical effects, game screenshot, website mockup, or UI. Generate one final illustration only.

Small incidental furnishings in the Archives image include plants, a wall map, and a framed harbor picture. They are atmospheric illustration details, not additional lore. Tiny incidental paper and spine marks are not intended as readable text.
